## 🚗 Introduction

Hello! This is the GitHub space for the Autonomous Mobility Control Lab (AMC Lab) led by Professor Weichao Zhuang. We are based at School of Mechanical Engineering, Southeast University.

Our research focuses on **autonomous mobility, intelligent vehicles, and vehicle control**. We are particularly interested in:

* Autonomous Driving Decision and Control
* End-to-End Autonomous Driving
* Extreme-Condition Vehicle Motion Control
* Cooperative Driving 

Our group aims to advance the intelligence, safety, and reliability of future mobility systems by integrating **artificial intelligence, vehicle dynamics, control, and robotics**.

We actively develop research methods, open-source tools, and experimental platforms for next-generation autonomous vehicles and intelligent mobility systems.

## 🌈 Contribution Guidelines & Collaboration

We actively contribute to the research community through publications, open-source projects, and academic collaborations.

* **Research Collaboration:** We welcome collaborations with universities, research institutes, and industry partners in autonomous driving, intelligent vehicle control, and cooperative mobility.
* **Prospective Students:** We welcome motivated Ph.D., Master's, undergraduate, postdoctoral, and visiting researchers interested in autonomous mobility and intelligent vehicles.
* **Using Our Code:** You are welcome to explore and use the code and resources in our repositories. Please cite the corresponding publications when appropriate.
* **Contributing to Projects:** For bug reports, discussions, or pull requests, please refer to the contribution guidelines of individual repositories.
